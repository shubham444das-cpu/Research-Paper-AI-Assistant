# 🧠 Research Paper AI Assistant

An intelligent AI-powered application designed to simplify the process of reading, understanding, and analyzing research papers.

The Research Paper AI Assistant allows users to upload research papers and interact with them through AI-powered features such as summarization, question answering, paper comparison, and citation analysis.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview

Research papers often contain large amounts of complex information, making them time-consuming to read and analyze.

This project uses Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing (NLP) to help researchers, students, and professionals quickly understand research documents.

Users can upload research papers and use the application to explore important information through an interactive and user-friendly dashboard.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 📄 Upload Research Papers

Upload research papers in PDF format and process them for AI-powered analysis.

### 📝 AI-Powered Summarization

Generate concise and meaningful summaries to quickly understand the important points of a research paper.

### 💬 Question & Answer

Ask questions about the uploaded research paper and receive intelligent answers based on the document content.

### 🔍 Research Paper Comparison

Compare multiple research papers and identify important similarities and differences.

### 📚 Citation Analysis

Explore and analyze citations related to research papers.

### 🧠 Intelligent Document Search

Search and retrieve relevant information from research papers efficiently.

### 📊 Interactive Dashboard

A clean and easy-to-use dashboard provides quick access to all major features.
